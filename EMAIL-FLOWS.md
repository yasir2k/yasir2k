# Klaviyo Email Flow Program — Hilason (Saddlery Inc.)

*Drafted 2026-07-13. Store: hilason.com. Audience after cleanup: ~13,344 subscribers + ~8,500 past customers (win-back).*
*Principle (high-value catalog): risk-reversal, financing, and service — NOT discounting saddles. Percentage-off is confined to accessories and the coldest win-back tier.*

> Setup note: Welcome + Abandoned Checkout are one-click templates in Klaviyo's flow builder — paste the copy below and set the timing. Nothing sends until Yasir approves the copy. Warm the sends: start with the engaged core, throttle the win-back.

---

## 1. Welcome series — trigger: joins a Klaviyo list (newsletter signup)
Goal: convert a new subscriber to a first order without discounting saddles.

- **Email 1 — immediately.** Subject: *"Welcome to Hilason — where the ride comes first"*
  Brand story (family-run, own-manufacturing, real horsemen), the fit-guarantee promise, link to best-sellers + the saddle fit quiz. Soft offer: **free shipping on accessories over $X** (not saddles).
- **Email 2 — +2 days.** Subject: *"Not sure which saddle fits your horse?"*
  The fit-confidence email: gullet/tree explainer, "Find your saddle in 60 seconds" quiz, plain-English exchange guarantee. Financing line: *"Saddles from $54/mo with Shop Pay."*
- **Email 3 — +4 days.** Subject: *"What barrel racers and trail riders reach for"*
  Social proof — top-reviewed products by category, a couple of customer photos/quotes. CTA to shop the categories they browsed (if known) or best-sellers.

## 2. Abandoned Checkout — trigger: Started Checkout, did not place order
Goal: recover the ~99% who leave. The single highest-ROI flow.

- **Email 1 — +1 hour.** Subject: *"You left something in your cart"*
  Show the exact item(s) + image, one-click return to checkout. Reassurance: secure checkout, easy returns.
- **Email 2 — +12 hours.** Subject: *"Still deciding? Here's the guarantee"*
  Lead with **risk reversal** (fit/exchange guarantee) + **financing** ("from $54/mo") — remove the two things that stall a considered purchase. Add 2–3 reviews of the abandoned product.
- **Email 3 — +24 hours.** Subject: *"Your cart's about to expire"*
  Urgency + a support offer ("questions about fit? reply to this email"). For **accessory-only** carts, a small first-order incentive is OK here; **never** discount a saddle cart — use the guarantee instead.

## 3. Post-Purchase / Replenishment — trigger: Placed Order
Goal: turn one order into a repeat customer; the biggest lever for the rising returning-customer base.

- **Email 1 — +1 day.** Subject: *"Thank you — here's what happens next"*
  Order reassurance, shipping expectations, how to reach a real person, care tips for the item.
- **Email 2 — +10 days (after delivery).** Subject: *"How's the [product] working out?"*
  Review request (feeds the on-site social proof) + cross-sell the natural companion (saddle → pad/cinch; vest → accessories).
- **Email 3 — predictive / replenishment window.** Subject: *"Time to restock?"*
  For consumables (fly masks, pads, grooming), fire on Klaviyo's predicted next-order date. This is where Klaviyo beats a basic ESP — real repeat revenue on autopilot.

## 4. Win-Back — trigger: past customer, no purchase in N months (the ~8,500 non-subscribed buyers, re-permission)
Goal: reactivate proven buyers. Run carefully, most-recent-first, small batches — protect sender reputation.

- **Email 1 — Subject: *"We've missed you at Hilason"***
  Lead with **what's new** (restocks, new arrivals) + the guarantee. Ask them to **re-confirm their subscription** (compliant re-permission). No discount yet.
- **Email 2 (non-openers, +5 days) — Subject: *"A little something to come back"***
  Coldest tier only: a modest, time-boxed accessory offer or free shipping. This is the one place a discount is warranted.
- **Sunset:** anyone who doesn't re-engage after this series gets suppressed — which also drops the Klaviyo billing tier. (This is the "keep buyers, then trim" plan we agreed on.)

---

## Rollout order (fastest revenue first)
1. **Abandoned Checkout** — biggest, fastest ROI. Turn on first.
2. **Welcome** — captures every new subscriber.
3. **Post-Purchase** — compounds with order volume.
4. **Win-Back** — run once, carefully warmed, then sunset.

## Guardrails
- No saddle discounts — guarantee + financing do the converting.
- Warm-up: engaged subscribers first; win-back throttled and most-recent-first.
- All copy needs Yasir's sign-off before first send.
- Success metric on the Monday Scorecard: **email = % of DTC revenue** (target 15% by day 90).
