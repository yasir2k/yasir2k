# Campaign Game Plan — Segment × Category × Season (Hilason DTC)

*Grounded in 2 years of Shopify DTC data (Jul 2024 – Jul 2026, ~$2.73M net, ~12–13k orders). Built by the CEO session 2026-07-20. Purpose: a repeatable playbook of who to target, with what, and when — the newsletter campaign was piece #1 of this.*

---

## 0. The five findings that drive everything
1. **~80% of orders are NEW customers** (returning-customer rate only ~13% in 2024 → ~20–24% now). **Retention is the single biggest untapped lever** — we acquire well but don't bring people back. Every repeat point on $2.7M is real money.
2. **DTC is saddle-led and high-AOV.** Western leather **saddles** are the revenue engine — Barrel-Racing flex-tree alone = **$368k**, plus Wade/Roping $136k, Treeless $223k, Trail/Pleasure $89k, Draft $75k. Accessories (headstalls, chaps, pads, cinches, vests) are the high-frequency layer.
3. **DTC ≠ Amazon in category mix.** DTC buyers = saddles + western apparel/tack (considered, high-AOV). Amazon = commodity horse supplies (fly sheets, blankets, protective gear). Different people, different playbooks.
4. **Q4 is the peak** (Nov–Dec: $164k→$200k in 2024; $125k→$135k in 2025). Aug–Sep strong (fall riding). **Jun–Jul is the trough.** Plan the calendar around this.
5. **Geographically concentrated in western/horse states** — **Texas #1** ($195k, 967 orders), then California, Florida, PA, MI, MO, OR, OH, GA, AZ. Enables regional + rodeo-event targeting.

## 1. Buyer segments (from the data)
| Segment | Who | Size/signal | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **First-time buyers** | Bought once | ~80% of orders | Convert to 2nd purchase = #1 opportunity |
| **Repeat/loyal** | 2+ orders | ~20% & rising | VIP treatment, highest LTV |
| **Lapsed one-timers** | Bought once, went quiet | thousands over 2yr | **Winback = biggest latent list** |

| **Trail/pleasure riders** | Trail/treeless saddles | $200k+ | Comfort/endurance angle |
| **Rodeo / bull riders** | Protective vests, chaps, bronc saddles | $80k+ | Safety + event timing |
| **Ropers / ranch** | Wade/roping saddles | $136k | Working-ranch angle |
| **Accessory/apparel** | Headstalls, chaps, belts, pads, girths | high volume, lower AOV | Frequency + cross-sell |
| **Newsletter non-buyers** | ~211 fresh signups | growing | Welcome→first purchase (built ✓) |
| **Browsers/abandoners** | Viewed/started checkout | now tracked (Klaviyo events live) | Automated recovery |

## 2. The campaign playbook

### A. Lifecycle flows (automated — set once, run forever)
| Flow | Trigger | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Welcome | List join | ✅ live | FIRSTRIDE, → first purchase |
| Abandoned Cart | Checkout Started | ✅ live & now firing | Events verified 07-20 |
| Browse Abandonment | Viewed Product | ⬜ **build next** (event now flows) | Big new opportunity |
| Post-Purchase Cross-Sell | Placed Order | ✅ live — **upgrade by category** | Saddle→pad/cinch/care; vest→chaps |
| 2nd-Purchase Nudge | 20–30 days post first order, no 2nd | ⬜ **build** | Attacks the 80%-new problem |
| Winback | 90–150 days no order | ⏸ held (deliverability) | Reactivate lapsed one-timers |
| Replenishment | Care/consumables ~60–90d | ⬜ build | Leather care, etc. |
| VIP | Hits 2nd/3rd order | ⬜ build | Loyalty perks, early access |

### B. Broadcast / segmented campaigns (the "campaigns" to run)
Priority-ordered:
1. **Winback the lapsed base** — reactivate 2 years of one-time buyers (subject to email consent — see §4). Biggest quick-win list. "We miss you + here's what's new." *Highest latent ROI.*
2. **New-customer 2nd-purchase / "complete your setup"** — cross-sell accessories to first-time saddle buyers (pad, cinch, headstall, leather care). Directly attacks the 80%-new gap.
3. **Discipline campaigns** (segment by what they bought/browsed):
   - **Barrel Racing** (biggest — lead here): saddles + pads + tack, timed to spring/summer show season.
   - **Rodeo / Protective** (vests, chaps): timed to rodeo season + safety messaging.
   - **Trail & Pleasure** (treeless/flex comfort).
   - **Roping / Ranch** (wade saddles, working angle).
4. **Q4 seasonal calendar** (the peak — plan NOW): early-bird → Black Friday/Cyber → holiday gift guide → last-chance. Biggest revenue window of the year.
5. **Seasonal category pushes:** Winter blankets early-access (**Aug**, ties to the winter initiative), Summer fly sheets (Amazon strength), Spring show-season.
6. **VIP / loyalty** to the ~20% repeat: early access, exclusive bundles, thank-you.
7. **Geo / event tie-ins:** Texas + western states; hooks around NFR, big barrel/rodeo events.
8. **Newsletter welcome offer** (first-purchase reactivation) — **built, in draft, awaiting greenlight.**

### C. Channels
- **Email** = the workhorse (segmented broadcasts + flows).
- **SMS** (Klaviyo SMS) = time-sensitive only (Q4 sale drops, back-in-stock, cart recovery) — opt-in required.
- **Amazon** (can't email those buyers — Amazon owns them): run the equivalent as **Sponsored Products by discipline/category** + seasonal (winter blankets, fly sheets). Category demand from Amazon informs DTC content.

## 3. Priority sequencing (what to run, in order)
1. **Now:** send the built **newsletter welcome** campaign (on greenlight) + build the **2nd-purchase** and **browse-abandonment** flows (events now flow).
2. **Aug:** **Winter blankets early-access** (DTC + Amazon) as stock lands; launch **Winback** to lapsed base.
3. **Sep:** **Discipline campaigns** (barrel racing first); build **VIP** + **Replenishment** flows.
4. **Oct–Dec:** **Q4 seasonal calendar** — the big one. Plan the sale/gift-guide sequence in September.
5. **Ongoing:** post-purchase cross-sell by category; geo/event hooks.

## 4. The one thing to verify before mass winback
Emailing the 2-year customer base depends on **how many are email-consented in Klaviyo** (Klaviyo was cancelled 2022→reconnected 2026; a prior suppression event flagged ~787 truly-engaged vs ~32k suppressed). **Action: verify the emailable/consented profile count** before planning winback volume — the strategy is right, but the addressable size sets the ambition. (SEO/Amazon campaigns have no such gate.)
